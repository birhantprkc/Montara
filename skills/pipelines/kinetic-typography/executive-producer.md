# Executive Producer - Kinetic Typography Pipeline

Use this pipeline when words are the visual system: lyric edits, manifesto pieces,
quote films, product launch titles, or social shorts where the viewer should feel
rhythm through text motion.

## Operating Rule

Keep the project text-led. If the brief becomes source-footage-led with only a few
labels, switch to `hybrid` or `talking-head`. If the brief becomes diagram-led,
switch to `animation` or `animated-explainer`.

## Stage Flow

1. `idea` - lock the text-led promise, target platform, and render_runtime choice.
2. `script` - create line groups with emphasis words and pause marks.
3. `scene_plan` - map lines to visual beats, holds, and transitions.
4. `assets` - resolve fonts, palette, audio, and optional background media.
5. `edit` - produce editable `edit_decisions` and Timeline IR handoff points.
6. `compose` - render through the selected runtime and run final review.
7. `publish` - package MP4, Timeline IR, copy, thumbnails, and editor exports.

## Send-Back Triggers

- Text lines are too long to read.
- No runtime selection decision is recorded.
- HyperFrames lint/validate is skipped when HyperFrames is selected.
- The output depends on generic cards rather than rhythm, hierarchy, and timing.
